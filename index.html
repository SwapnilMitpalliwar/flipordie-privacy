Flip or Die – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Flip or Die ("the game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is published by Swapnil Ashok Mitpalliwar ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or any account to play.
  • Your progress (scores, coins, skins, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game is free and shows ads. Our advertising and in-app purchase partners collect device information as described below.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (where available).

Information stored on your device

Best score, coins, unlocked skins, daily reward and challenge progress, achievements, purchases made and your settings are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: where required you are asked on first launch, and you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Tracking on iOS: the game asks for permission before accessing the IDFA. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Remove Ads: the optional purchase stops interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain optional.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold an account or your progress on our servers, most data is held by the providers above; you can exercise your rights with them directly or contact us and we will help. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and security

Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Third-party providers keep data according to their own retention policies (see their privacy policies linked above). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be noted in the game's store listing.
